# Handoff: joining and leaving

What to do when a designer joins or leaves this project. The goal: nothing the project knows lives only in one person's head or one person's Claude account.

## Joining

1. **Get access:** this repo on GitHub, the Figma files, and the Linear project. Ask the design lead for anything you can't open.
2. **Get the folder:** follow `SETUP.md` steps 2 and 3 (clone it, open it in Claude Code). Skip the setup interview. It only runs once, and it has already been done.
3. **Read `INDEX.md`.** It's one page.
4. **Ask Claude:** *"Give me a summary of this project and what's happening now."* If the answer is missing something important, that is a gap in the repo. Tell the design lead, or fix it.
5. **Talk to the design lead** about anything `INDEX.md` marks Unknown.

## Leaving (or rotating to another project)

About 30 minutes. It saves the next person weeks.

1. **Update `INDEX.md`:** where the project stands, what's in progress, what's blocked, and the key relationships on the client side.
2. **Check decisions:** anything you decided that exists only in a Figma comment, a chat message or your head? Ask Claude: *"Which decisions from the last month aren't written down in `decisions/`?"* Write them up.
3. **Check open questions:** is every question you're carrying in `product/open-questions.md`?
4. **Check meetings:** are the meeting notes you took in `research/meetings/`?
5. **Say "save my work".**
6. **Tell the design lead** it's done, so they can check `INDEX.md` reads well to someone new.
